ARES Rover & Arm Operator's Handbook

  • E-STOP Master Emergency Stop: Red header button immediately halts Rover drive motors AND freezes all Arm servo motion.
  • W, A, S, D Keyboard rover teleop: W (Forward), S (Reverse), A (Spin Left), D (Spin Right), SPACE (Halt).
  • HOLD / TOGGLE Switch between Hold-to-Move (safest, stops on button release) and Click-to-Toggle (continues till stop).
  • ARM LIMITS All 6 servos enforce conservative min/max angle limits. Angles shown are commanded open-loop angles.
  • FAILSAFE If communication drops for over 1.5 seconds, the ESP32 safety watchdog halts drive motors automatically.
